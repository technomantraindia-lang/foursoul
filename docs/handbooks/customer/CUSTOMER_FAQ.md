# Customer FAQ

**How do I register?** Open Register, complete the required fields, accept the legal terms, and submit.

**How do I find courses?** Open Courses from the navigation. Only published courses are listed.

**How is progress saved?** Lesson progress and completion are saved to your account while you learn.

**What happens when my subscription expires?** Protected paid lessons become unavailable, but progress, completed lessons, and history remain.

**How do I renew?** Open My Subscription and use Renew access when the option is shown. Payment-provider availability is displayed honestly on the page.

**How do I join a live session?** Open Live Sessions and choose a session during its permitted join window.

**Where is my certificate?** Open Certificates after completing the required course work.
