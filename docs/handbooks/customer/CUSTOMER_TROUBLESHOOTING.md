# Customer Troubleshooting

| Problem | Common reasons | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Cannot login | Wrong email/password or inactive account | Recheck credentials, reset password, or contact support. |
| Cannot open a lesson | Expired access, inactive entitlement, or unavailable course | Check My Subscription and course status; contact support if still blocked. |
| Progress is missing | Different account or unsaved recent activity | Confirm the login email and reload the lesson. |
| Cannot join live session | Outside join window or no course access | Check session time and subscription/course access. |
| Certificate unavailable | Requirements incomplete or review pending | Finish required lessons/assessment and wait for review where applicable. |
| Payment pending | Manual/offline confirmation is incomplete | Keep the order reference and contact support; pending payment grants no paid access. |
| Page shows an error | Temporary application problem | Record the URL, time, and message, then contact support without sharing secrets. |
