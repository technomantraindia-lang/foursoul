# FourSouls Customer Handbook

## Welcome

FourSouls gives you a place to browse courses, learn through lessons, track progress, complete assessments, attend eligible live sessions, and view certificates.

## Account and Dashboard

Register with your email, verify it when asked, and use **Login** to return. Your dashboard links to Courses, My Learning, Subscription, profile, security, preferences, and currency settings.

## Browse and Choose

Open `/courses` to browse published courses. Open a course to see its curriculum. Open `/plans` to review published subscription plans and prices in your selected AED, USD, or INR currency. Live payment is not currently available because a provider has not been configured.

## My Learning and Lessons

My Learning shows courses available to you and your progress. Open a lesson to watch its video, mark it complete, and move to the next lesson. Continue Watching uses your saved progress; expiry does not delete it.

## Quizzes, Assignments, Live Sessions, and Certificates

Open assessments from the course when access is available. Submit assignments through their course page. Live sessions appear when scheduled and accessible; joining is limited to the configured window. Certificates appear after the course requirements are satisfied.

## Subscription and Renewal

My Subscription shows your plan, status, dates, amount, currency, and access state. When access expires, protected lessons are locked but your history and progress remain. Use **Renew access** when shown. Renewal is currently an internal/manual flow pending payment-provider selection.

## When Something Goes Wrong

Check your login and account status first, then confirm the course is published and your subscription is active. Do not share passwords or payment details with support. See the [Customer Troubleshooting Guide](CUSTOMER_TROUBLESHOOTING.md).
